Our mission

To repair electronics properly, at component level, and to treat every customer honestly. We'd rather lose a job than take money for work that was never going to hold.

What we stand for

Six things we hold ourselves to on every job.

Honesty

We tell you what we find, even when it isn't what you want to hear. If something isn't worth repairing, we say so rather than take your money.

Integrity

We only take on work we believe has a reasonable chance of success, and we stand behind it with a lifetime warranty on our workmanship.

Transparency

Our prices are published. Every repair is photographed at each stage, and you get the pictures, whether the repair succeeds or not.

Right to repair

You should be able to repair what you own. We fix faults at component level, so devices other places would replace or write off get a second life, and fewer end up as waste.

Customer service

You deal with the people doing the work. We explain things in plain English, and we keep you updated from booking to return.

Your satisfaction

If anything doesn't seem right after a repair, tell us and we'll put it right. That's what the warranty is for.

What that means in practice

Promises are easy to make. These are the ones we've put in writing.

  • No surprises on price. We quote before any repair work, and nothing chargeable starts until you approve it. If it will cost more than you approved, we ask first.
  • No fix, no fee. If we can't repair your item, there's no charge for our time. You only pay return postage if we post it back.
  • A diagnostic fee only when there's something to diagnose. If the fault is obvious, like a broken port, we just price the repair.
  • A written account either way. If we can't repair it, we tell you what we tested and what we found.
  • Postage at cost. Return postage is charged at exactly what Royal Mail charges us, with no mark-up.
  • Lifetime warranty on our workmanship, and 6 months on replacement parts. Your statutory rights are never affected.
  • Terms in plain English. Everything above is written into our terms, so you know where you stand.
