Last updated: 20 September 2026

These terms apply to repair work carried out by Precision Electronics Repair, Watford, Hertfordshire. By sending us an item or booking a repair, you agree to them. Nothing here affects your legal rights as a consumer under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

1. What we accept

We repair phones, tablets, laptops, computers, games consoles, audio equipment, televisions up to 32 inches, and general circuit boards and small electronics.

We do not currently accept medical devices, vehicle safety systems (such as airbags or ABS), gas appliances or boiler controls, or any other safety-critical equipment. We may decline any item at our discretion.

Not every job carries a diagnostic fee. Where the fault is already clear — a cracked screen, a broken charging or HDMI port, a snapped connector — there is nothing to diagnose, so there is no diagnostic fee. We simply price the repair. We tell you before you send the item in whether a diagnostic fee applies to your job, and it is stated again on the booking form and on any quote we send you.

The diagnostic fee applies where we have to find the fault for you: the item does not power on, it behaves oddly, it has been in liquid, or it has been worked on before.

Our diagnostic fee covers up to two hours of bench time. If we have not identified the fault within that time we will treat the item as unrepairable, waive all fees except return postage, and return it to you with a written account of what we tested. We may choose to spend longer on an item, but we do not charge you for it.

Where we cannot repair an item, we will return it to you and explain what we found and what we tested.

3. Quotes and approval

We diagnose the fault before quoting. No chargeable repair work begins until you have approved the price. If we find that the repair will cost more than the amount you approved, we will contact you before continuing.

Parts are charged separately where they are needed, and are included in the price we quote you.

4. Your data and accessories

Please back up your data before sending or bringing in your device, and remove any memory cards, SIM cards, cases or accessories you do not want to send. We are not able to guarantee that data on a device will survive a repair, and some repairs cannot be carried out without risk to stored data.

Please tell us on your booking form about anything you do send with the item, so we can return it.

5. The nature of board-level repair

We repair items that are already faulty, and often already damaged. We take great care, document each stage with photographs, and only carry out work we believe has a reasonable chance of success. Even so, some faults cannot be repaired, and some items are beyond economical repair.

Where an item has previous damage, corrosion or earlier repair attempts by others, the chance of success is lower. We will tell you if we see this.

6. Warranty

  • Our workmanship: guaranteed for as long as you own the device.
  • Replacement parts: 6 months.
  • The warranty covers the specific repair we carried out. It does not cover the rest of the device, new or unrelated faults, accidental or physical damage, liquid damage after the repair, or work carried out by anyone else afterwards.
  • If you return an item under warranty and the fault turns out not to be covered, our £30 diagnostic fee applies where diagnosis was needed to establish that.
  • The warranty applies to you as the original customer and is not transferable.

7. Sending items to us

You are responsible for packaging your item securely and for the cost of sending it to us. We strongly recommend a tracked and insured service, as we cannot be responsible for items damaged or lost in transit before they reach us.

We will confirm receipt and record the condition of your item, with photographs, as soon as it arrives.

Batteries. Postal rules restrict lithium batteries. Loose batteries cannot be posted at all, and a battery that is swollen, leaking or otherwise damaged must not be sent. If your device has a damaged battery, please remove it and keep it, and tell us on your booking form. Parcels containing a device with its battery fitted must be handed in at a Post Office counter rather than posted in a postbox. Please check the current rules with your carrier before sending.

8. Payment and return

Payment is due once the repair is complete, before the item is returned or collected.

Your final bill is the repair price plus return postage. Prices we quote are for the repair. Return postage is charged at cost — exactly what Royal Mail charges us — and is added on top. It applies to every item we post back, whatever the outcome, including where you decline a quote or we cannot repair the item. If you collect in person by appointment, there is no postage to pay.
