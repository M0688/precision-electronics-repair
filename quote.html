Sending it in

There's nothing to reply to here. If you're happy to go ahead, just post the item to us. An address label and packing slips are attached to the email — print them, cut the label out and tape it to the parcel.

There's one packing slip for each item. Put each slip in with the item it belongs to — every item you want repaired needs its own slip. You can send them all in one box. The slip carries that item's job number, which is how we match it to your booking.
