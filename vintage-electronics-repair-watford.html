Common questions

How much does a vintage repair cost?

Price on application. Every vintage item is different, so we look at what it needs and quote individually. No chargeable work starts until you've approved the price.

Do you repair valve equipment?

Yes. We work on valve amplifiers and radios as well as transistor equipment.

Do you repair all makes and models?

Yes. Tell us the make, model and what it's doing when you book, and we'll tell you honestly whether it's worth repairing.

Can I post vintage equipment to you?

Yes, from anywhere in the UK. Pack it very carefully and secure any moving parts. We strongly recommend a tracked and insured service, as we can't be responsible for items damaged or lost before they reach us. Return postage is charged at cost.

What warranty do I get?

Our workmanship is guaranteed for as long as you own the item, and replacement parts for 6 months. It covers the repair we carried out. Every repaired item has a numbered warranty sticker, which needs to stay in place, and your emailed receipt is your proof of warranty. Your statutory rights are not affected.
