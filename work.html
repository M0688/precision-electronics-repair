Recent repairs

Component-level work on boards that other shops would replace or write off.

Controller drift — TMR stick upgrade

The original thumbsticks were swapped for magnetic TMR modules. Standard sticks drift because a contact wipes against a carbon track and wears it away. TMR modules sense position magnetically, with nothing touching, so the drift does not come back.

Gaming keyboard — USB-C port

The charge port was replaced and the damaged pads underneath rebuilt first, so the new port had something solid to sit on. Fit a port straight onto lifted pads and it comes off again.

PS5 — HDMI port replacement

The HDMI port was removed and replaced at board level — one of the most common console faults there is, and one that is often quoted as a whole-board replacement elsewhere.

Air conditioning control board

Power module, three-phase bridge rectifier and two resistors replaced. A board like this is usually quoted as a whole-unit replacement.

Air conditioning remote

Leaking batteries had corroded the terminals badly. Terminals replaced and the board cleaned up.

When it isn't worth repairing

Not every board can be saved, and pretending otherwise wastes everybody's money. When we find damage like this we say so, explain what we tested, and there's nothing to pay.

Not viable to repair

A vehicle body control module, assessed and sent back unrepaired. The damage was beyond what could sensibly be put right, so we said so and there was nothing to pay. We would rather lose the job than take money for work that was never going to hold.

No fix, no fee

If we can't repair your item there is nothing to pay — you get it back with a written account of what was tested and what we found. Our diagnostic fee covers up to two hours of bench time.
