How the postal service works

Tracked and documented from the moment it arrives.

1. Book online

Enter your name and email on our website and fill in the short booking form we send you. We reply with a free, no-obligation estimate.

2. Post your board

The estimate email includes an address label and a packing slip. Print them, pack the board carefully and send it to us from anywhere in the UK.

3. We confirm and photograph it

When it arrives we confirm receipt and photograph its condition, so there's a record from the start.

4. Diagnosis and quote

We find the fault and send you a price. No chargeable repair work starts until you approve it.

5. Repair, test and return

We repair it and test it, then post it back. Return postage is charged at exactly what Royal Mail charges us.

Before you post a board, try these first

Some faults have a simple fix. Worth a few minutes before you book a repair.

Equipment has no power

  • Check the fuse in the mains plug and try a different socket.
  • Check the power lead for damage, and any external power adapter.

Make sure it's the board

  • If you can, describe exactly what the equipment does and doesn't do when you book. It helps us confirm the fault quickly.

Stay safe

  • Don't remove a board from mains-powered equipment unless you know what you're doing. Capacitors can hold a dangerous charge even after it's unplugged.

Common questions

Do I need to be near Watford?

No. You can post your board to us from anywhere in the UK, and we post it back when it's done.

How should I pack a circuit board?

Put it in an anti-static bag if you have one, wrap it well so it can't move around, and use a sturdy box. We strongly recommend a tracked and insured service, as we can't be responsible for items damaged or lost before they reach us.

Who pays for postage?

You pay to send it to us. Return postage is added to your bill at exactly what Royal Mail charges us, whatever the outcome.

Do I have to pay a diagnostic fee?

Only if we have to find the fault for you. It's £30, and it comes off the repair price if you go ahead. If we can't repair it, there's no charge for our time, just the return postage.

Do you take work from businesses?

Yes. We repair boards for repair shops, installers and maintenance firms, as well as for the public.

What warranty do I get?

Our workmanship and any replacement parts are guaranteed for 12 months. Every repaired item has a numbered warranty sticker, which needs to stay in place, and your emailed receipt is your proof of warranty. Your statutory rights are not affected.
