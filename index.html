What we repair

If it has a circuit board, there's a good chance we can fix it. We diagnose the actual fault instead of just swapping parts.

Phones & tablets

Charging ports, no power, liquid damage and board faults.

Laptops & computers

No power, no boot, charging circuits and motherboard repair.

Games consoles

HDMI ports, power faults and controller repairs.

Audio equipment

Amplifiers, speakers and audio electronics.

TVs up to 32"

Power boards, main boards and no-picture faults.

Circuit boards

General board repair, microsoldering and BGA rework.

We don't currently repair: medical devices, vehicle safety systems (e.g. airbags, ABS), gas appliances or boiler controls, or any other safety-critical equipment.

You only pay a diagnostic fee if we actually have to diagnose something. If you already know what's wrong — a cracked screen, a dead HDMI or charging port, a snapped connector — there's nothing to work out, so there's no fee. We just price the repair. Parts are charged separately where needed. If we can't repair your item, there's no charge for our time — you only pay return postage. Where a diagnostic fee did apply and you decline the repair we've quoted, the £30 stands.

Prices are for the repair. Return postage is added at cost — exactly what Royal Mail charges us — on every job, whatever the outcome. Collect in person in Watford by appointment and there's no postage to pay.
