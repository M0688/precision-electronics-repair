PS5 no picture or no signal? What to try first

If your PS5 powers on but the TV says “no signal”, it's often the cable, the TV input or a video setting. Try these checks first. If none of them work, the HDMI port itself may be damaged.

Try these first

Work through these in order. Each one takes a few minutes.

  1. Check the TV input

    Make sure the TV is set to the HDMI input the PS5 is plugged into.

  2. Try a different HDMI cable

    Use a different HDMI cable, ideally the one that came with the PS5, and push it firmly into both ends.

  3. Try a different HDMI port or TV

    Plug into another HDMI input on your TV, or try a different TV or monitor if you have one.

  4. Power-cycle the PS5

    Turn it off, unplug it from the wall for a minute, then plug it back in and turn it on.

  5. Start in Safe Mode and reset the video output

    Turn the PS5 off. Hold the power button until you hear a second beep, about 7 seconds. Connect your controller with a USB cable and press the PS button. If you now get a picture, choose “Change Video Output” to reset the display settings.

Signs it needs a repair

If you've tried everything above, or you notice any of these, it's likely a hardware fault.

  • You get no picture on any TV, with any cable, even in Safe Mode.
  • The picture flickers or cuts out when you touch the cable.
  • The cable feels loose, or the port looks bent or damaged inside.
  • It stopped working after the console was moved or knocked with the cable in.

Common questions

Why does my PS5 say no signal?

Usually it's the TV input, the HDMI cable or a video setting. If it's none of those, the HDMI port on the PS5 may be damaged — it's one of the most common console faults.

Can a damaged PS5 HDMI port be repaired?

Yes. The port is replaced on the motherboard, and any damaged pads underneath are repaired first so the new port holds.

Is there a diagnostic fee for a broken HDMI port?

Not if the fault is obvious, like a visibly damaged port. We just price the repair.

No. We repair PS4, Xbox, Nintendo Switch and controllers too.
