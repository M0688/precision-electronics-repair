Spilled a drink on your laptop? What to do now

Liquid damage is one of the most repairable laptop faults, if you act quickly. What you do in the first few minutes makes a big difference. Follow these steps straight away.

Try these first

Work through these in order. Each one takes a few minutes.

  1. Unplug it and switch it off

    Pull the charger out first. If it's still on, hold the power button until it switches off. Don't wait to save your work.

  2. Don't turn it back on to check

    This is the most important step. Powering it while liquid is on the board is what causes short circuits and the real damage.

  3. Unplug everything else

    Remove USB drives, cables and anything else connected. If the battery is removable, take it out.

  4. Drain it

    Open it as wide as it goes and stand it upside down like a tent, so liquid drains out rather than further in. Wipe away what you can see with a dry cloth.

  5. Skip the rice and the hairdryer

    Rice doesn't dry the inside, and hot air can push liquid further in. Sugary drinks like coffee, tea and juice also leave a residue that drying won't remove.

  6. Get it looked at quickly

    Corrosion starts on the board even after it looks dry. The sooner it's cleaned and checked, the better the chance of repair.

Stay safe

If you see smoke or smell burning, unplug it straight away and keep it away from anything flammable.

Signs it needs a repair

If you've tried everything above, or you notice any of these, it's likely a hardware fault.

  • It won't turn on at all after the spill.
  • It turns on but the keyboard, trackpad or screen misbehaves.
  • It works only on the charger, or won't charge.
  • It worked at first, then failed a few days later. Corrosion can take time to show.

Common questions

Can a laptop be repaired after liquid damage?

Often, yes. We clean the corrosion off the board and replace the components that have failed, at board level, rather than replacing the whole motherboard.

It seems to work fine now. Do I still need to do anything?

It's worth having it checked. Residue left on the board can keep corroding it, and faults can appear days or weeks later.

Will I lose my files?

A board repair usually leaves the storage untouched, but we can't guarantee data survives. Back up as soon as you safely can.

How quickly should I send it?

As soon as you can. The longer liquid and residue sit on the board, the more corrosion there is to deal with.
