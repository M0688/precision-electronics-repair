Bluetooth speaker won’t turn on or charge? What to try first

A speaker that won't turn on or charge often has a simple cause: a worn cable, a weak plug or a blocked charging port. Work through these checks first. If none of them work, here's how to tell it needs a repair.

Try these first

Work through these in order. Each one takes a few minutes.

  1. Try a different cable and plug

    Borrow a cable and plug you know work. Cheap cables and plugs often can't supply enough power, and cables usually fail near the connector.

  2. Check the charging port

    Shine a torch into the port. Lint and grit stop the cable seating properly. Gently clear it with a wooden toothpick — never anything metal.

  3. Leave it on charge for an hour

    If the battery was completely flat, it can take a while before the speaker shows any sign of life.

  4. Charge it at room temperature

    Many speakers won't charge when they're very cold or hot. Bring it indoors and let it warm up first.

  5. Reset the speaker

    Most speakers have a reset, usually by holding a combination of buttons. The method depends on the model — check the manual or search for your model plus “reset”.

Stay safe

If the speaker is bulging, gets hot while charging, or smells of burning, stop charging it and don't use it. Contact us before sending it.

Signs it needs a repair

If you've tried everything above, or you notice any of these, it's likely a hardware fault.

  • It only charges if you hold the cable at an angle, or the cable feels loose.
  • Nothing happens with several known-good cables and plugs, and the port is clean.
  • It works only while plugged in, or dies within minutes of unplugging.
  • It stopped working after getting wet.
  • It turns on but there's no sound, or the sound cuts out.

Common questions

Can a Bluetooth speaker be repaired?

Often, yes. Common faults like a worn charging port, a failed battery connection or a fault on the power board can be repaired at component level.

Is it the battery or the charging port?

If the speaker shows no sign of charging with known-good cables, the port or the charging circuit is more likely. If it charges but runs out very quickly, the battery is more likely. We can check which.

Is it worth repairing a cheap speaker?

For a low-cost speaker, sometimes not. Tell us the make and model when you book and we'll tell you honestly if it's worth it.

Can I post my speaker to you?

Yes. A parcel with a battery fitted must be handed in at a Post Office counter, not put in a postbox. Don't post a speaker with a swollen or damaged battery — contact us first.
