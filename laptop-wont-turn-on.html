Laptop won’t turn on? What to try first

A laptop that won't turn on isn't always a dead motherboard. It can be the charger, a flat battery, or a screen fault that only looks like no power. Work through these checks first.

Try these first

Work through these in order. Each one takes a few minutes.

  1. Check the charger

    Most chargers have a light on the brick or the plug. If it doesn't come on, try a different wall socket. If you can borrow a charger with the right connector and wattage, try that.

  2. Leave it on charge for 30 minutes

    A completely flat battery can take a while before the laptop will start.

  3. Do a power reset

    Unplug the charger and anything connected, such as USB drives, docks and monitors. Hold the power button for 15–30 seconds. Plug the charger back in and try again.

  4. Check whether it's actually on

    Listen for the fan and look for any lights, such as caps lock. If it seems to be on but the screen is black, try the brightness keys.

  5. Plug it into a TV or monitor

    If you get a picture on an external screen, the laptop is running and the fault is likely the screen or its cable, not the motherboard.

  6. Undo recent changes

    If you've just added memory or another part, put the original back and try again.

Stay safe

If you smell burning or see smoke, unplug the laptop and don't try to turn it on again.

Signs it needs a repair

If you've tried everything above, or you notice any of these, it's likely a hardware fault.

  • The charger works on another laptop, but this one shows no lights at all.
  • It only charges if you hold the charging cable at an angle.
  • It turns on, then shuts off straight away.
  • It stopped working after a spill or a drop.
  • The charger light goes out when you plug it into the laptop.

Common questions

How do I know if it's the charger or the laptop?

If the charger's light stays on with another laptop, or a borrowed charger doesn't help, the fault is more likely in the laptop: the charging port or the power circuit on the motherboard.

Is a dead motherboard worth repairing?

Often, yes. Many no-power faults are one failed component, which we can replace at board level for much less than a new motherboard. We'll tell you honestly if it isn't worth it.

Will I lose my files?

A motherboard repair usually leaves the storage untouched, but we can't guarantee data survives. Back up first if you can.

I spilled a drink on it. What should I do?

Unplug it, switch it off and don't turn it on to check. Running it while wet is what does the real damage. Get it to us as soon as you can.
