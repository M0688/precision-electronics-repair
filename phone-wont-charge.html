Phone won’t charge? What to try first

Most phones that won't charge have a simple cause: a worn cable, a blocked port or a weak plug. Work through these checks first. If none of them work, here's how to tell it needs a repair, and what that costs.

Try these first

Work through these in order. Each one takes a few minutes.

  1. Try a different cable and plug

    Cables usually fail near the connector, and cheap plugs can't always supply enough power. Borrow a cable and plug you know work with another phone.

  2. Try a different socket

    Plug into a different wall socket. If you've been charging from a laptop or car, try a wall plug instead — those can supply too little power.

  3. Check the port for lint

    Shine a torch into the charging port. Pocket lint packs in over time and stops the cable going all the way in. Gently clear it with a wooden toothpick. Never use anything metal — it can short the pins.

  4. Leave it on charge for 30 minutes

    If the battery was completely flat, it can take a while before anything shows on screen.

  5. Restart the phone

    Do a force restart. The button combination depends on the model — search for your model plus “force restart”.

  6. Look for a moisture warning

    Many phones block charging if they detect moisture in the port and show a warning. Unplug it and let it dry fully before trying again.

  7. Check if it stops at 80%

    Many phones pause charging at around 80% to protect the battery. Check the battery settings before assuming it's faulty.

Stay safe

If the battery is swollen, the phone gets hot while charging, or you smell burning, stop charging it straight away and don't use it. Contact us before sending it.

Signs it needs a repair

If you've tried everything above, or you notice any of these, it's likely a hardware fault.

  • It only charges if you hold the cable at an angle, or the cable falls out.
  • Nothing happens with several known-good cables and plugs, and the port is clean.
  • The connector inside the port looks bent, loose or damaged.
  • It's been dropped or got wet, and stopped charging afterwards.
  • It charges, but very slowly or only when switched off.

Common questions

Why won't my phone charge even with a new cable?

The most common reasons are lint in the charging port, a worn port, or a fault in the charging circuit on the main board. Clean the port carefully first. If it still won't charge, it needs looking at.

Is it the battery or the charging port?

If the phone shows no charging sign at all with known-good chargers, the port or the charging circuit is more likely. If it charges but drains very fast, the battery is more likely. We can check which.

Will I lose my data?

A charging port or board repair can often keep the data, but we can't guarantee it. Back up first if you can.

Can I post my phone to you?

Yes. A parcel with a battery fitted must be handed in at a Post Office counter, not put in a postbox. Don't post a phone with a swollen or damaged battery — contact us first.
